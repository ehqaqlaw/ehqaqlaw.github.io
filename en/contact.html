Before you write

What to put in your first message

A short message is enough. At the start we need four things:

  • Your name, how you prefer to be contacted, and a good time.
  • The names of the other parties to the matter (a person or a company), so we can check for conflicts of interest before discussing any details.
  • A two-line description: what happened, and what you want to achieve.
  • What you are asking for: a written, video or in-person consultation; a proposal for a specific service; or a corporate counsel retainer for your business.
Do not send confidential details or documents in the first message.We ask for documents after the conflict check, through a channel we set for you, and they remain protected by professional confidentiality.
After your message

What happens next

01

Conflict check

We check the parties' names against our register before reading any details. If there is a conflict, we decline without going into the matter.

02

Setting the step

We suggest what fits: a consultation, an initial assessment, or a proposal for a defined-scope service, and we say what we need from you.

03

Written confirmation

The appointment or proposal is confirmed in writing, with scope, deliverables, timeline and fee, before you commit to anything.

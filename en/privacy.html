Privacy Policy

This is an informational website: it does not ask you to create an account, contains no forms, and does not collect your data. What you share with us is what you send us yourself, by telephone or email.

1. Who we are

Ehqaq Company for Law & Legal Consultations, a professional company, Commercial Registration 1131312936, Unified No. 7022093889, registered with the Saudi Bar Association. Our address: Ali Bin Abi Talib Street, Al Aman District, Building 3271, Buraydah 52385.

For enquiries about your personal data: privacy@ehqaqlaw.sa

2. What the website collects

Nothing from you directly. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies, nor analytics tools that identify you.

As with any website, the providers that run it receive technical data when you visit, including your IP address and browser type: the hosting provider (GitHub Pages), for operation and security, and the font service (Google Fonts), to load the site's fonts. Both providers are outside the Kingdom; we do not access this data and do not link it to you.

3. When you contact us

If you call or write to us, we use your name, your contact details and what you tell us about your matter to reply to you, and to confirm there is no conflict of interest before accepting any work. We do not use them for marketing, do not sell them, and do not share them except where the law requires.

Please do not send confidential details or documents in your first message. Your name, the names of the other parties and a two-line description are enough. Once we have confirmed there is no conflict and accepted your request, everything you provide is covered by the lawyer's professional duty of confidentiality under the Code of Law Practice.

4. Retention

We keep the correspondence of those who do not become clients for as long as needed to reply and to document the conflict check. Client files are kept for the period required by the rules of the profession and the anti-money-laundering regulations, which is at least ten years from the end of the engagement.

5. Your rights

You have the right to know what data we hold about you, to access it and obtain a copy, and to request its correction or the destruction of what is no longer needed, in accordance with the Personal Data Protection Law. Write to us at the address above. We may decline to destroy what the law requires us to keep, and we will tell you why. You may also lodge a complaint with the Saudi Data & AI Authority.

6. Updates to this policy

When booking and payment services are launched on the website, this policy will be replaced by an expanded policy explaining what is collected at that point and why, and the change will be announced on this page.

This page is an English translation of the Arabic original. The Arabic text prevails in case of any difference.

FAQ

Before you request

What is the difference between review and drafting?

Review is for a written contract prepared by someone else: we set out its risks and propose amendments. Drafting is for a contract not yet written: we write it in full on the basis of what you agreed with the other party.

Do you negotiate with the other party on my behalf?

Not within these services, which cover drafting, review and written revision rounds. Direct negotiation with the other party or its counsel is provided under a separate proposal setting out its scope and fees.

What if my contract is longer or more complex than the service scope?

You commit to nothing before we know. We read the document first and confirm that it falls within the service. If it goes beyond it, you receive a revised proposal explaining the reason, fees and timeline, which you may accept or decline at no charge.

Is the contract in Arabic only?

We draft in Arabic and provide a bilingual version under a separate proposal. We recommend stating that the Arabic text prevails in case of conflict, as Arabic is the language of litigation in the Kingdom.

Do my documents remain confidential?

Yes. We ask only for the parties' names at the outset, to run the conflict-of-interest check; documents are sent only after the request is accepted, and from that moment they are covered by the professional duty of confidentiality under the Code of Law Practice.

When does the delivery timeline start?

From your approval of the proposal and receipt of the required documents, not from the date of the request. If your reply to a query from us is delayed, delivery is postponed by the same period.

FAQ

Before you request

What is the difference between the articles of association and a shareholder agreement?

The articles are a statutory document registered with the competent authorities and available to third parties. A shareholder agreement is a private contract between you that details what is not usually written in the articles: funding, decision-making, exit and dispute resolution. The two must complement, not contradict, each other.

Is a shareholder agreement binding if it is not registered in the commercial register?

It is binding between its parties as a contract, and registration is not a condition of its effect between them. Its effect on third parties differs from that of the published articles, which is why we ensure the two do not conflict.

When does our company need a delegation matrix?

When more than one person in the company signs, approves or spends. The matrix defines who decides what and up to what limit, protecting both the company and its managers.

Do you handle incorporation procedures at the Saudi Business Center?

We prepare and review the documents and follow up the procedures before the authorities as agreed in the proposal.

We are in a dispute now. Where do we start?

By reading the articles and existing agreements; many disputes are already settled there. Then we set out the options: a written settlement, an orderly exit, or litigation. See Claims & Disputes.

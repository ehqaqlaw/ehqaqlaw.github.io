How we work

From request to delivery

01

Send your request

The parties' names and a brief description of the matter, with no confidential details or documents. We run a conflict check and reply. There is no charge at this stage.

02

Initial assessment

Once the request is accepted you send the documents, and we prepare a written assessment of your position and options, with a scope and fee you know in advance.

03

You decide the next step

You read the memorandum and choose: settlement, formal notice, litigation, or stopping here. Each step has its own proposal setting out stages and fees.

FAQ

Before you request

Do I have to file a lawsuit?

Not always. Many claims are resolved by a well-prepared formal notice or a written settlement. The initial assessment tells you whether litigation is worthwhile, and its prospects and cost compared with the alternatives.

How long does a commercial case take?

It varies with the court, the type of claim and the other party's position. We give you an estimate based on our experience after the initial assessment, and we do not guarantee a timeline.

How are litigation fees calculated?

They are set in the proposal according to the nature of the claim and its stages, and may be a fixed amount per stage or another formula agreed in writing. We state what the proposal covers and what it does not, and we ask for more only for work outside its scope.

I hold a cheque or promissory note. Do I need a lawsuit?

Cheques and promissory notes are among the instruments that can be enforced directly through the enforcement court without a substantive lawsuit, subject to conditions. See Enforcement & Collection, or send us the instrument to assess.

Do you guarantee the outcome?

No lawyer guarantees the outcome of litigation, and we do not promise one. What we commit to is an honest assessment of your position, work with the diligence of a careful lawyer, and keeping you informed at every step.

What if there is a conflict of interest?

That is why we ask for the parties' names at the outset. If a conflict appears we decline the request at no charge, and we keep no more of your data than our privacy policy requires.

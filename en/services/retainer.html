How we work

FAQ

Before you ask

How are hours counted?

The proposal sets the number of monthly working hours or requests, and how calls and reviews are counted. The monthly report details what has been used.

Do unused hours roll over?

That is set in the proposal; some retainers roll part over to the following month and others do not. We show you the option that suits the nature of your business.

Can the retainer be stopped?

Yes, in accordance with the termination terms in the contract, usually by prior written notice of a period set in the contract.

Who looks after our business?

A fixed responsible lawyer who knows your file, supported by the team where needed; the client is informed in advance of any change.

Does the retainer cover litigation?

No. Litigation and representation before authorities are provided under a separate proposal, and the retainer contract sets out how retainer clients' requests are handled.

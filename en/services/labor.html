FAQ

Before you request

Does a labour complaint start in court directly?

No. It is first submitted to amicable settlement at the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development; if it is not resolved within the prescribed period it is referred to the labour court. We represent you at both stages.

Is there a deadline for filing a labour claim?

Yes. The Labour Law sets a period after which a claim will not be accepted, counted from the end of the employment relationship, with exceptions. We therefore advise consulting a lawyer promptly after the relationship ends or when a dispute arises.

Do you represent both employers and employees?

Yes, but never in the same matter. We run a conflict-of-interest check before accepting any request, so we do not act for an employee against an employer we represent, or the reverse.

Must the work regulation be approved?

The Labour Law requires establishments to prepare a work regulation and have it approved through the channel prescribed by the Ministry. We prepare or review it in line with the law and the nature of your business.

Is advice before a termination decision really worthwhile?

It is usually the most economical step; an ill-considered termination can cost compensation and entitlements well beyond what a correct procedure would have avoided. The memorandum sets out the sound route and the risks before you decide.

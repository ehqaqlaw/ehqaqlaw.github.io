Before the appointment

How to get more out of your consultation

Prepare your documentsThe contract, correspondence or notices relating to the matter, even if they seem secondary.
Order your questionsBy importance; time is limited, and a specific question brings a more useful answer.
Mention the deadlinesAny hearing date or statutory deadline you know of; it may change the priorities.
Do not hide what you think is against youAn opinion based on incomplete facts is an incomplete opinion, and everything you say is confidential.
Ask about the next stepAnd its cost and timing, so that you decide knowing what lies ahead.
Keep the summaryThe summary or written opinion is your reference if you decide to proceed with us or with others.
How we work

From request to delivery

01

Contact us

By telephone or email: your preferred format, the area of the matter, the names of the other parties, and a two-line description with no confidential details.

02

Conflict check and confirmation

We run a conflict-of-interest check, then confirm in writing the fees and appointment, or the scope of the written consultation. No charge before that.

03

The consultation

You send your documents before the appointment. In the session the lawyer listens, asks, and sets out your position and options.

04

The summary and the next step

You receive a summary approved by the lawyer or the written opinion. If your matter needs further work, you receive a separate proposal to decide on.

Need more than a consultation?

If it turns out that your matter needs drafting, review or representation, we send you a written proposal after the consultation setting out scope, fees and timeline, and you may accept it or stop at the consultation.

FAQ

Before you request

Does a consultation create a lawyer-client relationship?

Yes, within its scope and once the request has been accepted and confirmed. Mere contact or a message sent before acceptance does not create that relationship, which is why we ask only for the parties' names at the outset.

Can I record the session?

A consultation may not be recorded without the lawyer's consent. Instead, you receive a written summary approved by the lawyer after the session.

What if my matter needs further work?

The lawyer tells you so in the session, and you then receive a separate proposal setting out scope, fees and timeline. Nothing obliges you, and the consultation stands on its own.

Is the consultation fee credited against later work?

That is set in the proposal you receive for the later work, and we state it expressly before you decide.

Do you advise on the laws of other countries?

No. Our services concern the laws of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia; where your matter involves another country's law we tell you so and confine our opinion to the Saudi aspect.

FAQ

Before you request

Is a lease registered on Ejar directly enforceable?

A contract registered on the Ejar electronic leasing network is treated as an enforceable instrument within the scope of its terms, so it can be submitted to the enforcement court for rent or eviction claims depending on its clauses. We review your contract and tell you what applies to your case.

My tenant is not paying. What do I do?

The route depends on whether the contract is registered, on its clauses, and on the correspondence between you. We start by studying the documents, then determine whether the route is directly through enforcement or through a lawsuit, and what needs to be documented first.

What should I check before signing a commercial lease?

The term and renewal, the rent increase mechanism, major and minor maintenance, the permitted use and assignment clause, termination events and their effect, and handover and vacating obligations. The review covers these clauses and others depending on your contract.

Do you review off-plan sale contracts?

Yes, within the sale contract review service, taking into account the guarantees and developer obligations that the competent authorities impose on these contracts.

Will you attend the title transfer or signing with me?

Attendance and representation in proceedings are not part of the document services and are provided under a separate proposal if you wish.

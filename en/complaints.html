Complaints

Every complaint is recorded and answered in writing, and is handled by someone other than the lawyer concerned.

How to make a complaint

Write to complaints@ehqaqlaw.sa, call +966 555 05 4041, or submit it in writing at the firm's office. State your name and contact details, the service concerned, what happened, and what you are asking for.

What happens next

We acknowledge receipt of the complaint within two working days, examine it and hear both sides, then reply to you in writing within ten working days. If you do not accept the reply, you may ask the managing partner to review it.

Confidentiality

Your complaint is handled confidentially and does not affect any current or future service to you.

External bodies

Making a complaint to us does not prevent you from approaching the competent authority: the Ministry of Justice, as the body supervising the legal profession, in matters of professional conduct; and the Saudi Data & AI Authority in matters concerning your personal data.

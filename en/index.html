How we work

Four steps, no surprises

01

Intake and conflict check

Your contact details, the parties' names and a brief description. We first confirm there is no conflict of interest before accepting your request.

02

Defining the scope

We complete the details and documents, and set out in writing what the work covers and what it does not.

03

Fee and schedule

You review the scope, fee and terms before any commitment; then we fix the schedule and you receive your invoice.

04

Delivery

We provide the consultation or the work, and you receive the agreed deliverable on time.

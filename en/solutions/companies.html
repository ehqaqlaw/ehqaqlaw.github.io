Corporate counsel retainer

How the retainer works

The retainer gives your business a responsible lawyer who knows its activity, its contracts and the history of its matters, with defined monthly hours agreed in writing:

  • Defined monthly hours for advice, document review and follow-up of ongoing matters.
  • One responsible lawyer for your business, supported by the team where needed.
  • A monthly report of what was done, hours used and what we recommend following up.
  • What the retainer does not cover — such as litigation and complex work — is offered under a separate proposal before it starts.
  • Cancellation by written notice, within the period agreed in the retainer.

FAQ

Before you request

Is the retainer suitable for a small business?

Yes, if it has recurring questions or contracts. If its needs are seasonal, defined-scope services fit better, and we say so plainly.

What if we exceed the monthly hours?

We tell you before the limit is reached, and anything beyond it is agreed in writing as the retainer provides.

Does the retainer include court representation?

No; litigation and complex work are offered under a separate proposal for each stage, so you know their scope and fees before they start.

Who sees our business information?

Only the responsible lawyer and the team members supporting them, under the duty of professional confidentiality and as set out in the privacy policy.

FAQ

Before you request

Do I need a lawyer for a small matter?

Not always. A written or video consultation covers many cases, and we tell you plainly if your matter needs nothing more.

Do you take personal status cases?

Yes. Divorce, khul', maintenance, custody and visitation, proof of marriage and divorce, estates and wills. It starts with a consultation in which we set out your position and options, then a written proposal if you need representation.

How do I know the fee before I commit?

Every service is set out to you in writing — scope, deliverables, timeline and fee — before you pay or sign anything.

Do you represent individuals in court?

Yes, within our practice areas, after an initial assessment that defines the position and the steps, with a written proposal for each stage.

Could you decline my matter?

Yes, if there is a conflict of interest or the matter is outside what we do well; we tell you at the outset without going into the details.

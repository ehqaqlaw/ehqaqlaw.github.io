Why Ehqaq

Four commitments, in every file

Clarity before commitment

We begin no work before you know in writing its scope, its deliverables, its fee, and what it does not cover. If anything changes, we tell you before it happens, not after.

Conflict check first

We ask for the parties' names before any details, and make sure we do not represent any of them. A professional obligation that protects the confidentiality of your matter and protects all of our clients.

Professional confidentiality

What you provide after your request is accepted is covered by the lawyer's duty of confidentiality under the Code of Law Practice, and does not end when the service ends.

Diligence, not promises

No lawyer guarantees the outcome of litigation or negotiation, and we do not promise one. What we commit to is an honest assessment of your position, work with the diligence of a careful lawyer, and keeping you informed at every step.

How we work

Four steps, no surprises

01

Intake and conflict check

Your contact details, the parties' names and a brief description. We first confirm there is no conflict of interest before accepting your request.

02

Defining the scope

We complete the details and documents, and set out in writing what the work covers and what it does not.

03

Proposal and approval

You receive a written proposal with the scope, fee and timeline, which you review before any commitment; work begins on your approval.

04

Delivery

We provide the consultation or the work, and you receive the agreed deliverable on time, with copies of everything filed.

The team

Licensed lawyers, in three cities, with one method

Ehqaq brings together founding partners and lawyers licensed by the Saudi Bar Association, in Buraydah, Riyadh and Tabuk. Every file is assigned to a responsible lawyer who knows its area, supported by the team where needed, and the client is informed in advance of any change in who handles their matter.

We state each lawyer's specialism in what they handle, and we do not accept work outside what we do well; if your matter falls in an area we do not cover, we tell you from the outset.
